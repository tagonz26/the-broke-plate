Quick Meals for Busy Students

When you have class, work and assignments all fighting for your attention, dinner needs to be simple. These meals can help you use what is already in your kitchen.

The "I Have Class in 20 Minutes" Plate

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Eggs
  • Leftover vegetables
  • Soy sauce or seasoning
  • Cooking oil

How to Make It

Heat a pan with a small amount of oil. Add your vegetables and rice, then push everything to one side. Scramble the eggs and mix everything together. Finish with soy sauce or your favorite seasoning.

The End-of-the-Week Rice Bowl

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Chicken, eggs or beans
  • Leftover vegetables
  • Salsa or hot sauce
  • Cheese, optional

How to Make It

Add warm rice to a bowl and top it with your choice of protein and leftover vegetables. Add salsa, hot sauce or cheese to bring everything together.

🛒 Shop Like You're Actually Broke

Before heading to the grocery store, check your refrigerator, freezer and pantry. Plan meals around ingredients you already own and look for foods that can be used in multiple recipes.

Buying one ingredient that works in several meals can help reduce food waste and make a small grocery trip go further.
