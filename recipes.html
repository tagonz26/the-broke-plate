Good food does not have to require a big grocery budget. These recipes are made for college students who want meals that are affordable, filling and actually worth making.

Quick Meals for Busy Students

When you have class, work, homework and approximately zero patience left, these meals are simple enough to get dinner on the table without draining your wallet.

The "I Have Class in 20 Minutes" Plate

A quick combination of rice, eggs and whatever vegetables or leftovers you already have in the refrigerator. It is simple, filling and easy to customize based on what needs to be used up.

What You Need

  • Cooked rice
  • 1 or 2 eggs
  • Any leftover vegetables
  • Soy sauce or your favorite seasoning
  • Cooking oil

How to Make It

Heat a little oil in a pan and cook your vegetables. Add the rice and seasonings, then stir everything together. Cook an egg separately or mix it directly into the rice. Serve hot and get to class.

The End-of-the-Week Rice Bowl

This is the meal for the night before grocery day. Use leftover rice, protein and vegetables to make something filling instead of ordering takeout.

What You Need

  • Cooked rice
  • Chicken, eggs or beans
  • Any leftover vegetables
  • Salsa or hot sauce
  • Cheese, optional

How to Make It

Heat your rice and protein together, then add whatever vegetables you have available. Top with salsa, hot sauce or cheese and serve in a bowl.

Meal Prep for People Who Hate Meal Prep

Meal prep does not have to mean eating the exact same container of food for five days straight. These meals use affordable ingredients and can be prepared ahead of time without making your week feel repetitive.

1. The "Caesar Student" Wraps

These chicken Caesar wraps turn a basic salad into a portable lunch. They are easy to prepare ahead of time and work well for class, work or a quick lunch between errands.

What You Need

  • Flour tortillas
  • Cooked chicken
  • Romaine lettuce
  • Caesar dressing
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Croutons, optional

How to Make It

Chop the chicken and lettuce, then mix them with Caesar dressing and Parmesan cheese. Add the mixture to a tortilla, top with crushed croutons if desired, and wrap tightly.

2. The "I Paid Rent This Month" Chicken Plate

Chicken, rice and canned green beans are a simple meal-prep combination that can stretch several servings without requiring expensive ingredients. It is filling, easy to portion and perfect for busy school weeks.

What You Need

  • Chicken breasts
  • Rice
  • Canned green beans
  • Lemon pepper seasoning
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper

How to Make It

Season the chicken with lemon pepper, salt and pepper, then cook it in a pan until fully cooked. Prepare the rice and heat the green beans. Divide the chicken, rice and green beans into containers for easy meals throughout the week.

3. The "Financially Responsible Taco Bowl"

Taco bowls are an easy way to turn inexpensive ingredients into several filling meals. Use ground beef, chicken or beans depending on what fits your grocery budget.

What You Need

  • Cooked rice
  • Ground beef, chicken or beans
  • Taco seasoning
  • Corn
  • Shredded cheese
  • Salsa

How to Make It

Cook your meat or beans with taco seasoning. Prepare the rice and divide it between containers. Add the meat or beans, corn, cheese and salsa. Refrigerate and reheat when you are ready to eat.

🛒 Shop Like You're Actually Broke

Before buying ingredients for a completely new recipe, check your kitchen first. Building meals around rice, tortillas, eggs, canned vegetables and other affordable staples can help you use what you already have and keep grocery trips cheaper.
