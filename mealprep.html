Meal Prep for People Who Hate Meal Prep

Meal prep does not have to mean spending your entire Sunday cooking twelve identical containers of chicken and rice. These ideas are simple enough for busy college students and flexible enough to keep meals from getting boring.

Watch: Chicken Caesar Wrap

Need a quick lunch idea? Check out this short chicken Caesar wrap video for an easy meal that can be prepared ahead of time.

The "Caesar Student" Wraps

Ingredients

  • Flour tortillas
  • Cooked chicken
  • Romaine lettuce
  • Caesar dressing
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Croutons, optional

How to Make It

Add chopped chicken, romaine, Caesar dressing and Parmesan to a tortilla. Add crushed croutons if you want extra crunch, then roll the tortilla into a wrap.

The "I Paid Rent This Month" Chicken Plate

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Rice
  • Canned green beans
  • Lemon pepper seasoning
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper

How to Make It

Season and cook the chicken in a pan until fully cooked. Serve it with rice and warmed vegetables for an easy meal-prep plate.

The "Financially Responsible Taco Bowl"

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Ground beef, chicken or beans
  • Taco seasoning
  • Corn
  • Cheese
  • Salsa

How to Make It

Prepare the rice and taco-seasoned protein. Add them to a bowl with corn, cheese and salsa. Divide into containers for easy lunches throughout the week.

Meal Prep Without Losing Your Mind

Choose ingredients that can work in more than one recipe. Cook enough rice or chicken for several meals, but change the seasonings and toppings so every meal does not taste exactly the same.
