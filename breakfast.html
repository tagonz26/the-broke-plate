Breakfast Without the Breakfast Price

Breakfast does not have to mean spending $12 at a coffee shop before your 9 a.m. class. These easy recipes use simple, affordable ingredients that can help college students start the day without starting their bank account crisis.

Breakfast for the Broke & Hungry

These breakfast ideas are designed for students who need something quick, filling and affordable before class.

Payday Chilaquiles

Ingredients

  • Tortillas
  • El Pato sauce
  • 2 eggs
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper

How to Make It

Cut tortillas into pieces and cook them in a pan with a little oil. Add El Pato sauce and toss until coated. Top with two cooked eggs and season with salt and pepper.

Five-Minute Fruit Flex

Ingredients

  • Rice cakes
  • Peanut butter or another spread
  • Banana or strawberries
  • Cinnamon or honey, optional

How to Make It

Spread peanut butter over the rice cakes and top with sliced fruit. Add cinnamon or honey if you want a little extra flavor.

8 A.M. Survival Quesadilla

Ingredients

  • 1 tortilla
  • 2 eggs
  • Shredded cheese
  • Oil or butter
  • Salt and pepper

How to Make It

Scramble the eggs and place them on a tortilla with shredded cheese. Fold the tortilla and cook it in a lightly oiled pan until both sides are golden.

Broke Banana Boat

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Peanut butter
  • Banana
  • Cinnamon, optional

How to Make It

Toast your bread, spread peanut butter over the top and add sliced banana. Finish with cinnamon if you have it.

Lecture Hall Parfait

Ingredients

  • Yogurt
  • Fruit
  • Granola or cereal
  • Honey, optional

How to Make It

Add yogurt to a cup or container and layer it with fruit and granola. Add honey if you want extra sweetness.

💸 The Broke Plate Rule

Before buying another specialty breakfast ingredient, check what you already have. Tortillas, bread, eggs, rice cakes and fruit can become several different meals when you mix and match them throughout the week.
