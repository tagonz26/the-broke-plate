Breakfast Without the Breakfast Price

Breakfast does not have to mean spending $12 at a coffee shop before your 9 a.m. class. These easy recipes use simple, affordable ingredients that can help college students start the day without starting their bank account crisis.

Breakfast for the Broke & Hungry

1. Payday Chilaquiles

Crispy tortillas, El Pato sauce and eggs come together for a filling breakfast that tastes way more expensive than it actually is. It is especially useful when you have leftover tortillas sitting in the fridge.

What You Need

  • Tortillas
  • El Pato sauce
  • 2 eggs
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper

How to Make It

Cut tortillas into pieces and lightly fry them in a pan with oil until crispy. Add El Pato sauce and toss until the tortillas are coated. Cook your eggs separately or right in the pan, then add them on top. Season to taste and enjoy.

2. Five-Minute Fruit Flex

Rice cakes are one of those cheap pantry staples that can turn into an actual breakfast with the right toppings. Add fruit and your favorite spread for something quick, crunchy and easy to take between classes.

What You Need

  • Rice cakes
  • Peanut butter or another spread
  • Banana, strawberries or other fruit
  • Cinnamon or honey, optional

How to Make It

Spread peanut butter over the rice cake and top with sliced fruit. Add cinnamon or a small drizzle of honey if you want extra sweetness.

3. The 8 A.M. Survival Quesadilla

When you have class in less than an hour, this is the kind of breakfast that gets the job done. Eggs, cheese and a tortilla make a filling meal without requiring much time or money.

What You Need

  • 1 tortilla
  • 2 eggs
  • Shredded cheese
  • Cooking oil or butter
  • Salt and pepper

How to Make It

Scramble the eggs in a pan. Place a tortilla in the pan, add cheese and the cooked eggs, then fold the tortilla in half. Cook both sides until golden and the cheese melts.

4. The Broke Banana Boat

Peanut butter and bananas are an inexpensive combination that can make basic toast much more filling. It takes only a few minutes and works especially well on mornings when you are running late.

What You Need

  • Bread
  • Peanut butter
  • 1 banana
  • Cinnamon, optional

How to Make It

Toast the bread, spread on peanut butter and add sliced banana. Finish with cinnamon if you want a little extra flavor.

5. The Lecture Hall Parfait

A simple yogurt parfait is an easy breakfast when you need something you can prepare quickly and take with you. Use whatever fruit is cheapest at the grocery store that week.

What You Need

  • Greek or regular yogurt
  • Fruit
  • Granola or cereal
  • Honey, optional

How to Make It

Layer yogurt, fruit and granola in a cup or container. Add a small amount of honey if desired. Keep it refrigerated until you are ready to eat.

💸 The Broke Plate Rule

Before buying another specialty breakfast ingredient, check what you already have. Tortillas, bread, eggs, rice cakes and fruit can become several different meals when you mix and match them throughout the week.
